ADHAYAN / introduction

An organization for learning in action

ADHAYAN is a youth-driven academic network built to connect curious minds, expand access to meaningful learning, and turn ideas into action.

We bring together students, educators, researchers, and organizations to learn beyond the classroom through academic communities, competitions, research, mentorship, and collaboration.

From discovering a new field to building something of your own, ADHAYAN creates the space, connections, and opportunities for young people to explore, create, and grow.

Mission

Our Mission

Establish a community that brings together researchers, students, developers, and innovators working in artificial intelligence and related fields from across the globe — and facilitate the exchange of research findings, methodologies, and insights among members of the international AI research community.

Vision

Our Vision

Build a genuinely international initiative with active participation from individuals across multiple countries and regions — supporting emerging researchers and practitioners in AI through mentorship, collaboration opportunities, and shared resources.

Founding objectives

What we stand for

  • International research collaboration Establish a community that brings together researchers, students, developers, and innovators working in artificial intelligence and related fields from across the globe.
  • Knowledge sharing Facilitate the exchange of research findings, methodologies, and insights among members of the international AI research community.
  • Capacity building Support emerging researchers and practitioners in AI through mentorship, collaboration opportunities, and shared resources.
  • Global community development Build a genuinely international initiative with active participation from individuals across multiple countries and regions.
