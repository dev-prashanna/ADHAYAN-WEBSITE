ADHAYAN: Founding Declaration & Timeline

ADHAYAN Founding Team September 01, 2026 Organization
#founding #history #declaration

ADHAYAN was established in Nepal on September 1, 2026, by the founding team of Pitambar Lamichhane, Prashanna Tiwari and Prince Kumar Mandal.

Founding Declaration

ADHAYAN was established in Nepal on September 1, 2026, following the founding meeting of Pitambar Lamichhane, Prashanna Tiwari, and Prince Kumar Mandal, who collectively initiated the organization as an international research and collaboration initiative focused on artificial intelligence and related fields.

The founding team subsequently began outreach and collaboration with individuals and prospective members across Nepal, Sri Lanka, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Papua New Guinea, Peru, and other countries with the objective of developing ADHAYAN as a global research community.
