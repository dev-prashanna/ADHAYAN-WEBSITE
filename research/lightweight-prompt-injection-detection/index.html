Lightweight Prompt Injection Detection: From Centroid-Based Embedding Classification to a Hybrid Guardrail

Independent research · Preprint

Prashanna Tiwari August 27, 2026 CC BY 4.0

Abstract

Prompt injection remains one of the most practical threats to large language model (LLM) applications. This paper presents a lightweight approach to prompt-injection detection, moving from centroid-based embedding classification toward a hybrid guardrail. We introduce Guardrailer v1, a dataset of 722,842 prompts, and show that a simple embedding-centroid classifier already captures a large share of injections, while a hybrid guardrail combining complementary signals improves robustness at low overhead. The method is designed to run close to the model with minimal latency, making it suitable for production guardrails.
